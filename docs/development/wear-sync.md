# Phone/Wear sync and offline alarms

Read `wear/build.gradle`, phone `WearSync`/`WearPhoneListener`, watch `PhoneSync`, `PhoneFallback`, `PhoneMirror`, `PhoneDismissalJob`, `WearWatchListener`, and shared `AlarmStore` before changing these flows. See [README Wear OS companion](../../README.md#wear-os-companion) for user-visible limits. Current behavior is version-specific; inspect both peers when changing a protocol.

## Ownership and snapshot acceptance

The watch has its own alarms. The phone owns synchronized phone alarms and groups; watch mutations require phone confirmation and must never silently become watch-local alarms. Data Layer requires compatible same-package/same-signer companions and a reachable peer. A sent message is not a receipt. Reassemble only bounded, complete snapshots; fence by owner node, publisher epoch, revision and publication ordering. Snapshot and acknowledgement can arrive in either order; UI confirmation requires the acknowledged owner/epoch and a snapshot at or beyond the committed revision. Do not hold store/identity locks across network waits. Reconnect and repair jobs must propagate persistence/acceptance failures and account for work arriving while a job runs.

For phone create/edit/delete, persist an uncertainty barrier before dispatch, bind receipts to the intended node/request/epoch and commit the receipt with the phone mutation. Do not automatically retry an uncertain create. A missing receipt is not proof of absence; bind negative status replies to their lookup attempt while accepting valid late positive receipts. Check the phone before explicit abandonment, warn about duplicates, and keep bounded non-evicting receipt capacity visible. Revision-checked edits may have committed even if the snapshot has not arrived. These guarantees belong to the companion protocol and do not give the external Binder API an idempotency key.

## Independent fallback is not mirroring

A cached list or a phone-ring mirror alone cannot alert when the phone dies before firing. The watch schedules separate device-protected fallback generations from the last accepted snapshot; offline edits/dismissals can leave them stale. Preserve the phone's first published occurrence instant across device zones and import both already-active one-shots and recurring occurrences without losing later recurrence. Do not suppress a watch trigger solely because the phone was recently reachable. Surface denied access, scheduling, capacity and pending-retry errors. The current fallback cap is 350 phone alarms; check source if changing limits.

`AlarmStore.syncPhoneFallback` validates the whole desired snapshot, persists it **with its owner** and a recovery wakeup before staging any registrations, and journals in-flight/obsolete generations. Retain the old working generation on replacement failure; commit before canceling obsolete PendingIntents. Recovery cleans staged tokens and retries desired state without the phone, even when an old token exists. Reject older revisions and retired publisher epochs, including when a desired replacement has not yet been applied. Bound old plus staged registrations and preserve native watch alarms.

Protect due, ringing and locally snoozed occurrences during replacement or owner/epoch change. Track active-token replay tombstones for both recurring and one-shot imports, including active occurrences observed while a local occurrence is protected; a dismissed recurring occurrence must not reappear, but its future recurrence must survive. Phone-to-watch occurrence-specific closure requires verified shared identity, not merely alarm ID, approximate time or connectivity. Histories and registrations are bounded; inspect failure paths rather than evicting safety records silently.

## Dismissal and other presentation

The current explicit watch-dismiss behavior is intentionally broader than occurrence matching: it requests dismissal of **all currently ringing** phone alarms, including unrelated ones. Snooze and automatic expiry do not send this generic request. The watch uses one request ID for the initial send and at most three retries aimed two seconds apart, stopping on valid acknowledgement; the request expires after 20 seconds. The phone atomically records the receipt with dismissal, including when nothing rings, so a replay cannot dismiss a later occurrence. Do not cancel future/snoozed alarms; a delayed first request can still affect alarms active when it arrives. Receipt capacity, clock skew and OS background limits matter. Local silence is not phone confirmation; outbox saturation must not block local dismissal. Trace local, fallback, mirror, notification, activity and gesture paths and guard cleanup against new sessions.

The watch complication reads committed local triggers and an advisory phone snapshot, not an independently guaranteed schedule. Unavailable, stale, unconfirmed, erroneous or pre-unlock state is unknown, not empty; keep uncertainty and timeline expiration conservative and avoid a provider/sync feedback loop. Watch faces may ignore timelines or truncate text. The experimental double-shake heuristic is app-local, not proof of an OEM gesture; scope listeners to ringing services, revalidate the exact occurrence and preserve acknowledgement semantics. Neither screen wake nor background Activity launch is guaranteed on Wear OS.

## Verification matrix

Exercise production code with disposable fakes: snapshot-before-ack and ack-before-snapshot; node/epoch replacement, stale revisions and replay; initial import while a one-shot or recurrence is active; due/ringing/snoozed preservation; local dismiss and recurrence tombstones; capacity, registration and durable-write failures. Inject death before/after desired-state commit, staged-journal writes, OS registration, replacement commit and obsolete cancellation; restart in a fresh process and verify exact owner, recovery wakeup, cleanup, retry without phone, and old-generation protection. Test duplicate/lost/delayed dismiss acknowledgements, no-active receipt, concurrent new sessions, outbox limits and expiry. Exercise complication uncertainty and notification/gesture routes separately.

A successful host probe or APK build does not verify physical offline delivery, reboot recovery, sound, vibration, watch wake, gestures, watch-face rendering or cross-device reliability. Device tests require explicit authorization; keep a trusted backup until verified.
